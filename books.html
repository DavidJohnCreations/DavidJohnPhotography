The Chronicles of Lemuria

Across centuries, civilizations rise and fall — but beneath history lies a deeper war.

In nineteenth-century Europe, a young Helena Blavatsky is chosen by a mysterious traveler from the distant future to recover fragments of ancient knowledge scattered across the world. What begins as a spiritual quest slowly reveals itself to be part of something far greater.

Centuries later, in the year 3029, scientists uncover a submerged temple bearing an impossible inscription: They find their own names on a plaque that says that they discovered time travel. As humanity rediscovers the ability to send consciousness — and eventually vessels — across time, a hidden organization known as the Constituency is revealed to have guided events for generations.

But the true origins of the conflict stretch back one hundred thousand years, to an ancient war fought with lightning-like weapons between time travelers and an alien race seeking survival through humanity itself. Their war destroyed Lemuria and the alien base on earth — which bound the souls of both aliens and time travelers to reincarnate throughout history.

Now, across lifetimes and reincarnations of both the alien souls and the souls of those time travelers called Lemurians, the war continues to be fought throughout time.

Will humanity ascend into higher consciousness…

or fulfill a darker design?

The Chronicles of Lemuria is a sweeping saga of time, reincarnation, hidden history, and the fate of human consciousness.

A Note From the Author

The Chronicles of Lemuria was originally conceived as a trilogy. However, as I began writing the first volume, the story expanded far beyond my original outline.


What was intended to be Book One became so large that I decided to divide it into multiple parts. As a result, The Adventures of Madame Blavatsky and the Guru from Nibiru serves as Part One of Book One in the larger Chronicles of Lemuria trilogy.


The edition currently available is being offered at a reduced price of $10 because it remains a working draft. While the story itself is complete in its current form, I am continuing to refine the manuscript through professional editing, revisions, illustrations, and audio production.


When the final editions are released through Apple Books, Amazon Kindle, and Audible, they will reflect the full scope of more than two and a half years of research, writing, and development, and will be priced accordingly.


For readers who choose to join me at this stage of the journey, thank you. Your support helps bring the larger vision of The Chronicles of Lemuria to life.


— David John






The Adventures of Madame Blavatsky and the Guru from Nibiru

The universe is not as it appears. In 1850s Europe, a young Helena Blavatsky is chosen by a traveler from the future to carry out a mission that spans centuries. Scattered across the world lie fragments of pre-Flood knowledge — hidden by secret orders, guarded by bloodlines, and forgotten by modern society. To prevent a future where the fate of humanity lies in the balance, Helena must rediscover ancient magic, assemble the lost Diagram of Enoch, and awaken truths that have long been forgotten.

Gaily bedight,
A gallant knight,
In sunshine and in shadow,
Had journeyed long,
Singing a song,
In search of Eldorado.


But he grew old—
This knight so bold—
And o’er his heart a shadow—
Fell as he found
No spot of ground
That looked like Eldorado.


And, as his strength
Failed him at length,
He met a pilgrim shadow—
‘Shadow,’ said he,
‘Where can it be—
This land of Eldorado?’


‘Over the Mountains

Of the Moon,
Down the Valley of the Shadow,
Ride, boldly ride,’
The shade replied,—
‘If you seek for Eldorado!’
-Edgar Allan Poe
