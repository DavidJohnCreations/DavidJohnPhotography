Description

Kitchen Spanish is a fast-paced bilingual party game inspired by the classic tradition of Lotería, designed to teach real-world kitchen and restaurant vocabulary through play.

Whether you work in the food industry, are learning Spanish, or simply enjoy game night with friends and family, Kitchen Spanish transforms language learning into an interactive social experience.

Each card features common restaurant and kitchen terms in both English and Spanish, paired with colorful imagery that helps players associate words with real objects used in everyday restaurant life.

From cuchara (spoon) and charola (tray) to lavaplatos (dishwasher) and caja para llevar (to-go box), players naturally build vocabulary while competing in a familiar bingo-style format that is easy for anyone to learn.

What makes Kitchen Spanish unique is its emphasis on speaking and pronunciation. Winning players must stand and say each word in both English and Spanish to claim victory, encouraging conversation, confidence, and participation from the entire group.

The game becomes not only entertainment, but a practical language-learning tool used through repetition, humor, and friendly competition.

Kitchen Spanish celebrates the language, humor, and teamwork found in kitchens everywhere while helping bridge communication gaps in a fun and memorable way.
